### Added

- **The main file EXIF in the asset detail and in the asset list sidebar.** The Info tab and the right panel of the asset list show a collapsible "Main file EXIF" panel under "File info", with the key and value of every EXIF entry. It shows for every image asset; an image without EXIF data shows 0 and the panel is disabled.

### Changed

- **Sorting of the asset list is a toggle on desktop.** The toggle shows every sort option at once and marks the active one. Small screens keep the dropdown.
