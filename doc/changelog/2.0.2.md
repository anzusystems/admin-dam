2.0.2 — 2026-08-17
===

### Fixed

- **The 2.0.1 redirects covered only three paths; now the whole rename is mapped.** 2.0.1 redirected
  `/asset/:id`, `/asset/file/:id` and `/user/:id/edit` — the paths that other apps link to — but a
  bookmark to a list or detail page, such as `/asset` or `/author/12`, still landed on the not-found
  page. All 20 renamed roots now redirect, including the segment that was renamed one level deeper
  (`/podcast/:id/episode/:episodeId` → `/podcasts/:id/episodes/:episodeId`). Paths that 2.0.0 left
  alone (`/login`, `/logout`, `/settings`, `/close-page`, `/unauthorized`) are untouched.
