2.5.0 — 2026-11-25
===

Needs core-dam-bundle 1.62.0 and common-admin 1.53.0.

### Added

- **Default source and enforced single use in the licence form.** The licence form and detail show the default source, picked only from sources, and the switch "Vynútené jednorazové použitie". New and updated files of such a licence are always single use. Needs core-dam with `defaultSource` and `flags.singleUseEnforced` on the licence.
- **A "1×" badge on single use assets.** The badge shows on the tile and in the Info column of the table row of the asset list. It shows when the main file of the asset is single use.
- **Priority in credit of an author and a source.** The edit form has a number from 0 to 100000, the detail shows it and the lists of authors and sources have it as a column. A higher number comes first in the credit of a photo, equal numbers keep the order.
- **Badge of a source.** The edit form and the detail of a source have a badge of up to 4 characters A-Z and 0-9. The asset list shows the badges of all sources of a photo after the licence badge, in the order of the credit priority.

### Changed

- **The automatic deletion date only in the asset detail.** The asset tile and list row no longer show the badge with the remaining time. The file info shows the date with the remaining time under the shorter label "Zmaže sa", so the label no longer runs into the date.
- **The image preview picker offers no single use photos.**

### Fixed

- **The "1×" badge updates after saving metadata.** Saving metadata in the list sidebar or in the asset detail sidebar updates the badge of the saved asset in the list without a reload. The other assets of its take over group update after a reload.
- **The upload queue keeps the saved single use.** Saving an asset opened from the upload queue passes the new value to the queue, so a later save of the queue does not send the old one.
